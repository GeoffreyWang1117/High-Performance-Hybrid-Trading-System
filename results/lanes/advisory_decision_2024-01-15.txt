VERDICT
  NOT RESOLVED. The median (-0.0102) is inside two standard
  deviations of the run-to-run spread (0.0255), so this
  configuration does not measure the policy -- it measures
  thread scheduling. Whether an advisory happens to be fresh
  when a toxic trade arrives depends on when the slow lane
  last woke.

  Do not quote a single run's number. To get a measurement,
  reduce the dependence on timing: raise --ttl-ms so advice
  stays valid across slow-lane wakeups, or lower --speed so
  the slow lane publishes more often per unit of market
  time.

  15.4% of advisories were rejected as expired or
  generation-stale. That is the design refusing to act on
  outdated model output, not a malfunction -- but a policy that
  is ignored most of the time cannot show much effect either
  way.
