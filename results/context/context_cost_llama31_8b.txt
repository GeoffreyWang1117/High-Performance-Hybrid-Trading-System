  READ THE TWO COLUMNS APART. Every arm answers the SAME 400
  decision points, so the trend down each column is a within-sample
  comparison and is the result. The absolute level is not comparable
  with Part A: those points were stratified on the trade following
  the context, which enriches the delivered sample in a way a
  full-tape number is not subject to.

  at context   +0.0650 -> +0.1100  across 128x more context
  delivered    +0.2361 -> +0.0559  across 413 ms -> 2887 ms of age
